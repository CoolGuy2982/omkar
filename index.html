About

Hi, I’m Omkar. I’m from Mechanicsburg, Pennsylvania and I’m studying computer science and artificial intelligence at Purdue (class of 2029).

Since August 2025 I’ve been a researcher in Purdue’s CATALYST lab, working with Prof. Sanjay Rebello and Dr. Amogh Sirnoorkar on an NSF-funded project about using AI to improve how students learn physics.

Outside of research I’m working on Prism and CalcGPT, and I’m on the vertical farm team in Purdue’s VIP program.

AAPT Summer Meeting 2026

In July 2026 I presented our research and gave an oral talk at the American Association of Physics Teachers Summer Meeting in Pasadena, California.

Research

  • Students’ Epistemological Beliefs and their Chatbot Preferences in AI-mediated Physics Learning Amogh Sirnoorkar, Omkar Mamidpalliwar. arXiv, 2026.

We had intro physics students work through an online waves module with a chatbot, let them choose how the chatbot tutored them (guided questions, direct answers, or a mix), and looked at how that choice related to their beliefs about physics knowledge.
